Public release policy: allowlisted first-party research, checkpoints, source, reports, tokenizer/config and provenance metadata. No general model license has been selected. IHMS-derived implementation explicitly attributes CC-BY-NC-4.0; complete upstream license and modification notice preserved. HMS/IHMS reference clones and papers are source-URL/commit/hash metadata only.

REDISTRIBUTABLE_PUBLIC: first-party symbolic LatticeReason v1/v2 synthetic reservoirs (generators inspected; no natural-bank input), first-party scientific outputs and authored code, source/provenance metadata.

MANIFEST_ONLY / UNKNOWN_DO_NOT_UPLOAD: DATA-D natural text/tokenized arrays; BabyLM; FineWeb/FineWeb-Edu/FineMath/Wikipedia raw/tokenized corpora; retokenized natural subsets; natural banks; benchmark examples and contamination registry. Existing source manifests, hashes, builder scripts, source URLs and certificates are retained. Tokenization does not grant redistribution rights. Corpus-source mailto URLs in metadata were reviewed as false secret positives.

Archives: only nested source archives passing secret/license screening are allowed. Archives containing unreviewed benchmark contents are omitted; their source Git revisions and loose audited research trees are preserved. No research originals overwritten or deleted. Exact per-file omissions are in LOCAL_ONLY_OMISSIONS.json.
